# V9.4 First-Time User Test

Give the site to someone who has not seen The Merit Register before. Do not explain the interface first.

Ask them to complete these tasks:

1. Find IGIMS Patna.
2. Check what an AIR 8,000 OBC candidate can reach.
3. Compare any two colleges.
4. Save one college to My List.
5. Find where they would make a final counselling order.
6. Find the Junior Culture research only if they want deeper evidence.

Record where they hesitate, what labels they misunderstand, and any control they fail to notice. A hesitation of more than ~5 seconds on the homepage is a UX bug for this release.

## V9.4 design target
The first screen should communicate three tasks without explanation: search a college, check rank reach, compare options. Advanced research and planning tools should be discoverable but not demand attention.
