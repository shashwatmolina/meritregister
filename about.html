About the project

College choice should be more than a cutoff table.

The Merit Register combines counselling data with sourced college intelligence: clinical exposure, academics, research, hostel and campus life, current student culture and the practical trade-offs that shape five and a half years of MBBS.

How the evidence is handled

Official institutional material is preferred for hard facts. Current student accounts are used for lived experience and are labelled as such. Conflicting reports remain conflicting. Unknown information stays unknown.

Junior Culture evidence is intentionally separated from the calibrated college score, and anonymous allegations are not treated as verified institutional facts.

Counselling data

2026 Round-1 values are provisional. Quota streams are kept separate; AIQ, Open Seat, DU, IPU, ESI and other routes are never silently merged.

Deep profiles

Thirty high-interest colleges use the standardized deep dossier, and all 30 are marked refreshed in the current synchronized research queue.

Junior Culture

Tracks rulebook claims, intro/PDP culture, ground rules, hostel exposure, opt-out evidence, incidents, official response and the Freshers Experience Timeline.

Data Quality

Measures how complete and diverse the evidence is, then surfaces research gaps. It ranks research need, not college safety.
