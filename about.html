Project overview

About this project

The Merit Register is a student-facing directory and counselling reference for government MBBS colleges in India. The current build combines a nationwide searchable directory, 2026 seat intake, MCC/AIQ cutoff layers, an AIQ rank predictor, demand trends, structured hostel research and a deep college-comparison workspace.

02 / Counselling data

Round-wise cutoffs where available

College detail panels can show category-wise MCC/AIQ closing AIRs and demand history. Missing rounds remain missing rather than being converted into a made-up cutoff.

03 / Decision tools

Built for actual choice filling

The site includes an AIQ rank predictor, searchable filters, detailed college modals and a separate comparison workspace for turning raw data into actual college decisions.

What the directory currently contains

  • a nationwide government/public MBBS college directory
  • state, city, institution type and management
  • establishment year and 2026 MBBS seat intake
  • distinct tags for AIIMS, ESIC, Central, JIPMER, AFMC, State and ASMC colleges
  • new-2026 tags for colleges added this year

What the counselling layer does

The embedded counselling layer is MCC/AIQ-focused. Depending on the college, the detail panel can show General/OBC/EWS/SC/ST category data and Round 1, Round 2 and Round 3 closing AIRs. The predictor reports the earliest round in which the entered AIR reaches a college.

Deep comparison coverage

The comparison workspace currently has 30 researched college profiles across clinical exposure, academics, research/international pathway, campus life and finances. Ten of those also have exhaustive hostel reconstructions.

What is still incomplete

Evidence depth remains uneven. AIQ coverage varies by college, only part of the directory has deep non-counselling research, and fees/hostel/student-life information can change by batch or year.

How to use the site

Start with the AIQ predictor if you want a quick view of colleges your rank reaches. Use the directory to search or filter the full register. When you have two to four serious options, open Compare colleges for a structured side-by-side view of counselling, clinical exposure, academics, research, hostel, campus life and finances.

How the cutoff data is represented

Cutoffs are shown primarily as closing All India Rank. For round-wise MCC/AIQ data, a dash means no fresh or upgraded allotment for that college-category combination was recorded in that round; it should not be read as a zero or an inferred cutoff. Where only a narrower or older source layer exists, the site keeps that limitation visible rather than pretending every college has the same depth of evidence.

The project is increasingly based on direct counselling reconstructions and official-source cross-checks, but not every embedded record has the same provenance. For an admission decision, the relevant MCC or state counselling authority remains the final source of truth.

Project intent

The goal is to make a fragmented counselling landscape easier to inspect. Official portals are authoritative, but they are usually organised around notices, seat matrices and allotment PDFs rather than around the question a student is actually asking: what colleges exist, how many seats do they have now, what happened in recent rounds, and how should I organise my choices?

What is intentionally not included

The site does not invent missing cutoffs or fill weakly sourced college-life fields merely to make every profile look complete. Official facts and student-reported experience are kept distinct, and a blank or Unknown field is intentionally allowed to remain a data gap.

It also does not replace the current MCC seat matrix, allotment result, state counselling notice, NMC recognition data, or the rules published by the relevant counselling authority.

Use The Merit Register as a research and choice-organisation layer. Before locking choices or acting on a cutoff, verify the relevant year, quota, category and round against the official counselling source.

Current limitations

The directory is broader than the deep-research layer. AIQ/cutoff coverage and evidence depth differ from college to college, and only 30 institutions currently have the full comparison research pass. Treat the site as an evolving evidence-backed research build, and verify counselling rules, current fees and official notices before acting.
