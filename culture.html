What actually happens after admission?

Junior Experience & Senior Culture

This layer tracks reports of informal fresher rulebooks, “intro” systems, appearance or movement expectations, senior-junior hierarchy, positive mentoring, dated disciplinary cases and the institution’s formal anti-ragging response. The First 90 Days layer asks what happens in the first weeks, whether hostellers and day scholars differ, whether boys/girls report different experiences, what happens if students opt out, and what changes after freshers. The new Freshers Experience Timeline then places only phase-supported claims into Joining → Weeks 1–4 → Months 2–3 → Freshers → Rest of first year → Second year, leaving unsupported periods blank. A Reddit post is evidence that a claim was publicly made — not proof that every student experienced it.
